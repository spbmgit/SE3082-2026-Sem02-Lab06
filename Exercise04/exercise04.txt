Exercise 4 - parallel for and collapse

Part 1: pragma omp parallel for
Only the outer loop (r = 1 to 5) is split across threads. Each thread then runs the full inner loop (k = 1 to 10) on its own. 
With 2 threads: thread 0 got r = 1,2,3; thread 1 got r = 4,5 (30/20 split).

Part 2: pragma omp parallel for with collapse
Both loops are merged into one set of 50 iterations, which OpenMP splits evenly across threads instead of just the outer loop. 
This gave a 25/25 split, cutting through the middle of r = 3 rather than along the outer loop's natural boundary.

Conclusion: collapse gives OpenMP more iterations to divide (50
vs 5), producing a finer, more balanced split of work.
